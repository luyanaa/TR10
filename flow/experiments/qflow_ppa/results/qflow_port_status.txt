TR10_cmp_sc qflow port status (attempt 2)

Synthesis:
  Yosys 0.52 (system) segfaults reading the TR10 Liberty.
  Yosys 0.70 (LibreLane nix store) reads it and maps the FIFO:
    23 DFF, 22 MUX2, 6 BUF_X1, 14 INV_X1, ... (131 placed components)
  qflow's legacy vlog2Verilog antenna-cleanup step then segfaults, so the
  mapped netlist (results/qflow_synth_mapped.v) is used for placement.

Placement:
  Native GrayWolf placement of the qflow-synthesized netlist: 7 rows,
  59.4 um pitch, die 491.4x432 um (row span), no channel.
  GENR*numrows=7 + rowSep=178.2 -> die 577.8 x 138380 um; cell y up to
  79.8 mm.  qflow's rowSep/numrows units/row model is unusable as-is.
  A post-shifted 237.6 um row pitch was used for the router experiment.

Routing (qflow-synthesized netlist, 5.4 um grid, qflow LEF):
  qrouter failed 77/118 nets, many with "no tap points":
    _194_/CK of clk, _175_/A of mem3[1], count[0], _62_, ...
  This indicates a qflow netlist/DEF pin-name mismatch for the mapped
  DFF/MUX2 cells, in addition to clock congestion.

Routing (LibreLane-mapped netlist, 5.4 um grid, qflow LEF):
  9/118 failed: edff_rstb_tie (TIEHI HI M1 pin touches VDD), clk,
  rd_ptr/wr_ptr, and a few _* nets.

Remaining blockers for a complete port:
  1. qflow legacy vlog2Verilog segfault / netlist cleanup.
  2. qflow netlist-DEF pin-name mapping for DFF/MUX2 (77 no-tap nets).
  3. GrayWolf rowSep/numrows row model (huge die; no 237.6 pitch).
  4. TIEHI HI must have a separate M2 signal pad + V1 (currently M1
     pin touches VDD; qrouter sees no signal tap).
  5. clk is a multi-row net; needs clock tree/NDR or clock-aware routing.
  6. qflow qrouter grid is 5.4 um with off-grid cell pins; fine grids
     (2.0/1.0/0.1 um) do not finish in 20 min.

Conclusion: the port has a working qflow tech script/LEF and working
Yosys 0.70 synthesis, but qflow 1.4's legacy netlist/row/router tools are
not compatible enough with TR10_cmp_sc to produce a complete route.
No valid qflow PPA yet; OpenROAD remains the complete PPA baseline.
